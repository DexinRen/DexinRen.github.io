## Ingredient List

| Name                                  | Amount | Necessity | Notes                                            |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Chicken breast                        | 1000 g | Required  | Water-boiled, hand-pulled into thick strips      |
| Sesame paste                          | 25 g   | Required  | Prefer Chinese roasted sesame paste (not tahini) |
| Roasted Chili oil                     | 50 g   | Required  | With sediment preferred                          |
| Vinegar (Zhengjiang Aromatic Vinegar) | 15 g   | Required  | Chinese mature vinegar or white rice vinegar     |
| Soy sauce                             | 75 g   | Required  | Adds umami and salt                              |
| Sugar                                 | 25 g   | Required  | White or rock sugar, dissolves better if warmed  |
| Sichuan pepper (ground)               | 1.5 g  | Optional  | Ground; numbing layer, add at end                |
| Scalian (white stem, minced)          | 25 g   | Optional  |                                                  |
| Roasted Sesame Oil                    | 10 g   | Required  |                                                  |
 
---
## ⚠️ Notes
- **Chicken prep**: Instead of whole bone-in chicken, use water-boiled chicken breast. Cook until just done (75 °C internal), rest, and shred into strips **along the grain**.
- **Sesame paste**: Thick pastes should be thinned using **warm water or soy sauce**, stir slowly in one direction to avoid clumping.
- **Sauce layering**: Mix sesame paste with soy sauce and sugar first, then add vinegar, chili oil, garlic, and finally pepper powder last. Stir until smooth.
---
## 🧑‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

| Step | Description                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Boil chicken breast in salted water until just cooked through. Let cool and shred by hand into strips.                                                    |
| 2    | In a bowl, mix sugar, soy sauce, and vinegar until sugar dissolves. Stir in sesame paste slowly, adding warm water 1 tsp at a time if needed to smoothen. |
| 3    | Add chili oil (with sediment) and optional garlic. Stir until sauce is creamy and homogeneous.                                                            |
| 4    | Place shredded chicken into a bowl, pour sauce over. Top with ground Sichuan pepper and optional herbs. Do not mix if serving traditionally.              |
| 5    | Serve cold or room temperature as appetizer or side dish.                                                                                                 |

## 💡 Characteristics

麻 (numbing), 辣 (spicy), 甜 (sweet), 咸 (salty), 酸 (sour) — **all five flavor notes present**.  
Great with alcohol or as a refreshing contrast in a hot meal.

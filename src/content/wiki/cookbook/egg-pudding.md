## Ingredient List

| Name        | Quantity    | Stage | Note                                                                                                |
| ----------- | ----------- | ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Egg         | 3           | 2     | Beaten and mixed with salt and broth                                                                |
| Salt        | 1 g         | 2     | To season the egg base                                                                              |
| Soup stock  | 150-300 g   | 2     | Clear stock (pork/chicken broth), or water with umami enhancer. lesser the soup, denser the pudding |
| Pork (lean) | 100 g       | 1     | Minced or finely chopped, skinless                                                                  |
| Winter veg  | 25 g        | 1     | Optional: finely chopped dongcai                                                                    |
| scallion    | 25 g        | 1     | Chopped finely                                                                                      |
| Soy sauce   | 25 g        | 1     | Adds base umami to the meat topping                                                                 |
| Sesame oil  | A few drops | 1     | Finish/aroma                                                                                        |
| Lard/oil    | 40 g        | 1     | For stir-frying the pork topping                                                                    |

---
## Instructions
1. Finely mince pork (with some fat), and rinse winter vegetables; chop them and scallion finely.
2. Heat wok or pan, add oil/lard, stir-fry minced pork until water evaporates and aroma emerges.
3. Add chopped winter veg, soy sauce and scallion; stir-fry till aroma emerges and veg change colors
4. Beat 3 eggs evenly with salt. Slowly stir and add cold broth (can use starch water as a substitute, but you have to do a final stir when egg mixture turn hot but not turn pudding yet, to maintain the suspensoid states)
5. Strain the egg mixture through a fine sieve into a bowl to remove bubbles.
6. Gently place the bowl in steamer. Use **High heat** to steam for 5 minutes (or wait until it gelatinized. in a typical family kitchen, 15 min is expected).
7. add the cooked topping evenly on top, with optional drops of sesame oil.
---
## Notes
- keep **water-to-egg ratio at 1:1.5 to 2:1 by volume**, lesser the soup, denser the pudding.
- Strain the egg mixture to get rid of bubble, this give the pudding smooth texture

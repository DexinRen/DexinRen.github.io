## Ingredient List

| Name                       | Amount       | Necessity | Notes                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Tofu (firm)                | 500 g        | Required  | Cut into ~2 cm cubes, blanch before use       |
| Ground pork                | 100 g        | Optional  | Prefer half-lean half-fat, finely minced      |
| Vegetable oil              | 75 g         | Required  | Used to fry pork and aromatics                |
| Doubanjiang                | 50 g         | Required  | Fermented chili-bean paste, central to flavor |
| Salt                       | 2 g          | Required  | Adjust depending on salinity of doubanjiang   |
| Sichuan pepper             | 1 g (ground) | Required  | Add at the end for maximum aroma              |
| Garlic chives or Scallions | 75 g         | Optional  | Cut into 3–5 cm strips, added at the end      |
| Fermented black beans      | 10 pcs       | Optional  | Lightly mashed before use                     |
| Soy sauce                  | 25 g         | Optional  | Enhances umami                                |
| Chili powder               | 20 g         | Optional  | Add based on desired spiciness                |
| Corn starch                | 40 g         | Required  | Used to thicken the sauce, see warning below  |
| Broth/Stock                | ~400 g       | Required  | Pork, chicken, or light vege stock            |

---
## 🍳 Cooking Steps

| Step | Description                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Cut tofu into ~2 cm cubes. Blanch in lightly salted boiling water for 30–60 seconds. Drain well.                                   |
| 2    | Mince pork. Cut garlic chives into 3–5 cm strips. Lightly mash fermented black beans.                                              |
| 3    | Heat oil in wok over medium-high. Add pork, stir-fry until fragrant and just cooked.                                               |
| 4    | Add doubanjiang, fermented black beans, and optional chili powder. Fry until oil turns red and aroma is rich (do not burn).        |
| 5    | Add broth/stock, soy sauce, salt (if needed). Bring to simmer. Add tofu. Gently push tofu to avoid breaking. Simmer 3–5 minutes.   |
| 6    | Add garlic chives and stir gently. As soon as chives soften and turn color, **slowly stir in starch slurry** until sauce thickens. |
| 7    | Remove from heat. Sprinkle ground Sichuan pepper before serving. Serve hot with rice.                                              |
## ⚠️ Notes & Warnings

- **Starch thickening warning**:  
    To avoid forming gelatinized clumps ("starch pudding"):
    - Reduce starch amount to ~40–50 g.
    - Always **add the starch slurry slowly**, **while stirring**, **before broth is fully reduced** (when the wok still contains visible liquid).
    - If the sauce is too dry, add more stock or water before thickening
- **Tofu blanching is critical**:  
    - Briefly boiling the tofu before stir-frying helps to firm texture, remove beany odor, and prevent breaking.
    - Gently push the tofu in the wok with the non-sharp back side of the utensils, to avoid smashing the tofu

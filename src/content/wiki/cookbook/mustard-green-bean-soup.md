## Ingredients
| Name                                      | Weight | Necessity |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- |
| Dried Board/Lima Bean                     | 250 g  |           |
| Dry-Fermented Mustard Green                | 150 g  |           |
| Scallions                                 | 30 g   | Optional  |
| Lard                                      | 30 g   |           |
| Pork/Chicken Bone Broth (milky)           | 1 kg   |           |
| Salt                                      | 5 g    |           |
| MSG/Chicken Bouillon Powder/Yeast Extract | 1.5 g  | Optional  |
| Pickle Brine (Sour Enhancer)              | 150 ml | Optional  |
## Cooking Ｍethod
| Step | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Soak the dried beans in water for at least 12 hours, wait until the seed coat softens and can be peeled easily                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 2    | Remove the seed coat and split each bean in half.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 3    | Slice the mustard greens into 0.5 cm wide strips. Gently squeeze out excess brine — until they stop dripping, but do not over-dry                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4    | Cut the scallions into thin rings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 5    | Heat a wok over high heat                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6    | Add lard and heat until fully melted and hot                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 7    | Add the mustard greens and stir-fry until a savory aroma develops                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 8    | Add the split beans and stir-fry with the greens for 5–10 minutes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 9    | Pour in the broth. Add salt. Reduce to **medium-low heat** and simmer for at least 15 minutes — until the broth becomes milky (especially if only using water), the soup absorbs the pickle flavor, and at least half of the beans break down into mush. This creates a creamy texture while retaining some whole beans for contrast. |
| 10   | Turn off the heat. Add scallions and your choice of MSG, bouillon, or yeast extract.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 11   | Optionally add some pickle brine or more salt to enhance sourness and umami taste                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Note
- For best flavor harmony and depth, **use pork bone broth** (milky) — it integrates smoothly with the lard to enrich the soup’s mouthfeel and aroma.
- **You may substitute water for broth** (you’ll lose the “background taste”) and **cooking oil for lard** (you’ll sacrifice aroma development). This results in a lighter soup with reduced complexity and a flatter aromatic profile.
## Warnings
- Do **not** use fresh (green) beans. They will discolor the soup, turning it a dark greenish shade, and alter the expected taste and appearance. Always use **dried** white or light-colored board/lima beans for the correct texture and creamy broth color.
- **The seed coat must be removed.** Leaving it on ruins the mouthfeel by adding a thin film-like texture, and it prevents the beans from breaking down into the desired creamy consistency during simmering.

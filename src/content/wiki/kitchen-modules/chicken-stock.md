### Ingredient
| Name              | Quantity (g/ml) | Notes                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chicken bones     | 1500–2000 g     | Back, neck, wings, carcass preferred                                                                                       |
| Cold water        | 3000–4000 g     | Enough to submerge the bones fully                                                                                         |
| Onion             | 200 g           | Quartered, skin-on okay                                                                                                    |
| Celery            | 120 g           | (~2–3 ribs) Cut into chunks                                                                                                |
| Carrot            | 150 g           | (~1–2 medium) Rough chopped                                                                                                |
| Bay leaf          | 0.3 g           | Optional                                                                                                                   |
| Thyme             | 1 g             | Optional (1–2 sprigs)                                                                                                      |
| Parsley stems     | 3 g             | Optional                                                                                                                   |
| Black peppercorns | 1 g             | Optional, whole only                                                                                                       |
| White vinegar     | 15–20 g         | Optional, use **ONLY IF** you want more gelatin in soup; Add **before heating**; rest bones in it for 30 min in cold water |

---
## 🔧 Instructions
1. **Preparation**
    - Rinse chicken bones under cold water to remove blood or debris.
    - Optionally blanch: cover bones with water, bring to boil for 2 min, discard water, rinse bones.
2. **Simmering**
    - In a large stock pot, add bones and **cold** water (enough to submerge).
    - Bring slowly to a bare simmer, skimming off scum that rises.
    - Add vegetables and aromatics.
3. **Cooking**
    - Maintain a **low simmer** (never boil) for **4–6 hours** (or 8+ for deeper flavor).
    - Skim occasionally to keep the broth clear.
4. **Straining**
    - Remove large solids with a slotted spoon.
    - Strain broth through fine mesh or cheesecloth into a clean pot or container.
5. **Cooling & Storage**
    - Cool quickly (ice bath or portion into small containers).
    - Store refrigerated up to 5 days, or freeze for 3 months.
---
## 🧊 Notes
- **White stock (clear, clean):** skip roasting the bones.
- **Brown stock (deep flavor, dark color):** roast bones and aromatics at 200 °C until browned
- Best modular base for **western sauces, stews, gravies**, or diluted into soups.
- **DO NOT salt.** Salting is done **per dish** when used.

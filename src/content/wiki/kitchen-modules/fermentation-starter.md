## Ingredient
| Name        | Amount       | Stage | Notes                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| water       | 1kg          | 1     | Clean, None-chlorinated                                                                                        |
| starch      | ~90g         | 1     | Potato, corn, or rice starch                                                                                   |
| sugar       | 3–5 g        | 1     | Optional, accelerates LAB growth                                                                               |
| Ginger      | 1 slice      | 1     | Optional, suppress yeast & other microbiotic                                                                   |
| LAB habitat | samll amount | 1     | LAB habitat (Cabbage outer leaf, carrot/radish skin, or no-preservatives pickle juice), **DO NOT WASH/CLEAN ** |
| Salt        | 15 g         | 1     | ~1.5%, allows LAB to dominate                                                                                  |
| Salt        | 35 g         | 2     | Halt fermentation, boost saline concentration to 5% by weight                                                  |
| sugar       | 2-3 g        | 2     | OPTIONAL. Add only if PH not reach 3.9-4.0 after the 3rd day                                                   |
## Tools
- PH Tester (3-7)
- Temperature Measure
- Jar with lid (best with single-direction valve)
## Instructions
1. **Growth medium**
    - Dissolve starch, sugar (stage 1), and salt (stage 1) in water
    - Stir to get a suspensoid (make sure sugar & salt is **fully dissolved**). 
    - Stir it with low to mid heat, the suspensoid start to gelatinize at about 70–80 °C
    - Stop heating once the suspensoid turn semi-transparent, with glue-like stickiness & consistency (starch is now half gelatinization and will not precipitate, starch network is now established)
    - Wait until it cool down to room temp (25 Celsius), then add LAB habitat inside (don't have to add a lot, it just need to introduce LAB to the growth medium)
    - Check initial PH value $k_{0}$
2. **Storage & Fermentation**
    - Pour the growth medium into a jar and enclose it using a lid with single-direction valve (can use a towel or let a normal lid loosely closed, but single-direction valve is the best)
    - Pump out the air inside the jar (if using single-direction valve, don't need to be a vacuum, just try pump air out until hard to do so)
    - Store the jar under room temperature (**22-28 Celsius**, best at 25 Celsius) in a dark environment (avoid sunlight or UV light, or steal it in a black plastic bag)
    - Let sit undisturbed for **3–7 days**, (Optional) shake or stir, without removing the lid, once a day to prevent surface mold
    - At the end of the third day (72 h), check: 1. PH value $k_{t}$ ; 2. If there is any visible bubbles in the gelatinized solution (small amount of bubble is okey); 3. Smell & taste (if smells normal) the solution.
	    - If $k_{t}\geq 4.0$, do:
		    - if $k_{t}=k_{0} \lor k_{t}>6$: LAB not introduced, add new LAB habitat if the medium smells & taste normal, with no bubble observed
		    - If $k_{t}\in[5,6], k_{t}\neq k_{0}$: Continue fermentation process for 2 extra day, then check PH again.
		    - If $k_{t}\in(4,5], k_{t}\neq k_{0}$: Continue fermentation process for 1 extra day, then check PH again. Consider adding 2nd stage sugar at this time, if fermented over 72 h.
		- If **excessive** bubble observed, check the smell of the solution, if smells like alcohol or other non-pickle or non-water smell, **dispose** this batch and start over (Yeast or other non-LAB microbiotic dominant) 
		- If it smells not like water or pickle (A hint of ginger is fine), **dispose** it and start over
		- If $k_{t}\in[3.9,4.0]$, and smell/taste sour (no alcohol or rot smell), the fermentation is **complete**. Its now officially a fermentation starter (for pickle), aka inoculant, and you are safe to move to step 3.
3. **Halt Fermentation and Preserve**
	- Add stage 2 salt to the inoculant, this brings up saline concentration to ~5%, and stop almost all microbiotic activity
    - Store in fridge (DO NOT FREEZE), to further slowdown the microbiotic activities.
    - Use as **10–20% volume** inoculant in new vegetable pickle brine

A light, **fast-extracted savory liquid**, focused on **aromatic profile** and **clarity**, used in soups, stir-fry dishes, noodles, and sauce deglazing. Contains soluble amino acids and light fats but **low gelatin**.

---
### 📝 White Chicken Broth — 鸡白汤 (Modular Clean Base)
### Ingredients

| Name                             | Quantity   | Necessity | Notes                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ---------- | --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Chicken parts (bone-in, skin-on) | 1 kg       | Required  | Whole chicken, carcass, or wings/backs  |
| Cold Water                       | 2.5–3 L    | Required  | Enough to submerge chicken              |
| Ginger                           | 1–2 slices | Optional  | Adds subtle aroma, suppresses meat odor |

---
### Cooking Method

| Step | Description                                                                                                       |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | (Optional but recommended) Blanch chicken in boiling water for 1–2 min to remove blood & impurities. Rinse clean. |
| 2    | In a clean pot, add chicken + cold water. Optionally add ginger slices.                                           |
| 3    | Heat over medium-high until just at boiling point. Skim all scum and foam.                                        |
| 4    | Immediately reduce to low heat. Simmer gently **without bubbling** for 1.5–3 hours.                               |
| 5    | Strain. Optionally skim off surface oil.                                                                          |
| 6    | Cool and store. Refrigerate up to 3 days, freeze up to 1 month.                                                   |

---

## 🧱 Note: Modular Strategy

This broth is the **most modular liquid base** in Chinese and fusion cooking:
- Flavor-neutral: no herbs, no strong aromatics
- Maximum flexibility: adapt to **any cuisine direction**
- High control: aromatics can be added **during cooking**, not during extraction
## 🧂 Ingredients (Chinese Variant)

| Name                | Amount    | Note                                   |
| ------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Chicken (with skin) | 400–500 g | Bone-in thighs, backs, or half carcass |
| Water               | 1 kg      | Cold start for clarity                 |
| Ginger              | 1 slice   | Removes rawness                        |
| Scallion            | 1 stalk   | Aromatic top note                      |
| Salt (optional)     | 2–3 g     | Only if used standalone                |

## 🧂 Ingredients (Western Variant)

| Name    | Amount                   | Note                        |
| ------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Chicken | same as above            | Can include wings or thighs |
| Onion   | 1/4 piece                | Optional sweetness          |
| Carrot  | ~30 g                    | Adds light color            |
| Celery  | ~20 g                    | Optional body               |
| Herbs   | 1 bay leaf / thyme (opt) | Do not overdo it            |

---

## 🔥 Instructions

1. Add chicken and aromatics to pot, cover with cold water.
2. Bring to boil over **medium** heat. Skim foam as it forms.
3. Once boiling, reduce to **low simmer** (small bubbles) for **45–60 minutes**.
4. Strain through fine mesh. Skim off fat for clear broth (or keep for richness).
5. (Optional) Salt if used standalone.

---

## 🧊 Storage

- Refrigerate: 3–4 days  
- Freeze: 1–2 months  
- Store in 200 mL blocks for modular use

---

## 📝 Notes

- Broth is **not gelatinous** — do not overboil.
- Used as base for **noodle soup**, **quick stir-fry**, or **light poaching**.
- Do **not reuse bones** for second batch.

## Ingredients

| Name                                  | Amount | Necessity | Notes                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Sesame paste                          | 25 g   | Required  | Prefer Chinese roasted sesame paste (not tahini)  |
| Roasted Chili oil                     | 50 g   | Required  | With sediment preferred                           |
| Vinegar (Zhengjiang Aromatic Vinegar) | 15 g   | Required  | Chinese mature vinegar or white rice vinegar      |
| Soy sauce                             | 75 g   | Required  | Adds umami and salt                               |
| Sugar                                 | 25 g   | Required  | White or rock sugar, dissolves better if warmed   |
| Sichuan pepper (ground)               | 1.5 g  | Required  | Ground; numbing layer, add at end                 |
| Roasted Sesame Oil                    | 10 g   | Required  |                                                   |
| Scalian (white stem, minced)          | 25 g   | Optional  | Add only when using the sauce, it has to be fresh |
## Cooking Ｍethod
Mixing them together, add scalian only when using this sauce, since it should be served fresh.
## Notice
The formula can be used to season 1000g of meat/noodle/vege. It works the best with chicken breast, and noodle 

Since this is easy to make based on existing ingredient, no complex cooking method required, and its a unstable suspensoid, it is not recommended to pre-make this sauce. Better to mix and add to the dish on the go.

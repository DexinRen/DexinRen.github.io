## Ingredients (Standard Batch)

| Ingredient | Weight Ratio             | Typical Amount                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Onion      | 2 parts                  | 200 g                          |
| Carrot     | 1 part                   | 100 g                          |
| Celery     | 1 part                   | 100 g                          |
| Fat        | ~10–15% total veg weight | 30–45 g butter/Tallow/lard/oil |

---
## 🍳 Cooking Instructions

1. **Preparation**
    - Peel and finely dice the **onion** and **carrot**
    - Trim and dice the **celery stalks**
    - Keep the dice size **uniform** (around 5 mm cubes)
2. **Heat fat**
    - In a **wide, heavy-bottomed pan**, melt **30–45 g of fat** (butter, lard, or neutral oil) over **medium-low heat**
3. **Sweat the vegetables**
    - Add **onion** first. Stir 1–2 min until translucent edges appear.
    - Add **carrot** and **celery**. Stir to coat all evenly in fat.
    - Reduce to **low heat** and **sweat** (cook gently without browning) for **15–20 minutes**
    - Stir occasionally. The vegetables should become soft and aromatic, **not browned**
4. **Finish**
    - Remove from heat. Use immediately, or cool and portion into airtight containers for freezing.
---
## Freezing Tips
- Cool completely.
- Portion into **ice cube trays**, silicone molds, or ziplock bags (100 g each).
- Label with date and fat type.
- Keeps for **up to 3 months** in freezer.

## Common Usage

|Dish Type|How to Use Mirepoix|
|----|-----|
|**Vegetable or meat stock**|Simmer raw or sautéed mirepoix in water or bones|
|**Stews and braises**|Add at the beginning to build flavor foundation|
|**Gravies or sauces**|Sauté with roux or deglazing liquid|
|**Soups**|Start with mirepoix before adding main ingredients|
|**Risotto or pilaf**|Sauté mirepoix before adding rice|
|**Roasts**|Use as a bed under meat for moist baking and sauce base|

## Fat Substitution Notes

|Fat Type|Use Case|
|---|---|
|**Butter**|French sauces, risottos, poultry dishes|
|**Lard**|Pork dishes, rustic braises, red meats|
|**Beef tallow**|Beef stews, hearty soups|
|**Vegetable oil**|Vegan/neutral dishes|
